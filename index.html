Cartographic foundations & disclosure

Every world is generated in the browser from a seed: a warped-noise continent, mountain spines with ridged relief, priority-flood drainage that carves rivers and fills lakes, climate-driven biomes, least-cost roads between settlements and generated place names. Coastlines, contours, rivers and coast rings are true vectors, so exports stay crisp at print size. Terrain range shaping adapts Azgaar’s Fantasy Map Generator under the MIT license; hillshade and hypsometric practice is informed by Eleanor Lutz’s Atlas of Space. The renderer, terrain model and symbol library are original. The globe stand is modelled in Blender and staged with three.js (MIT, loaded from jsDelivr); the engraved gores are generated in the browser in the manner of 18th-century terrestrial globes.

AI disclosure: the cartography engine in this build was rewritten with Claude (Anthropic) working from the earlier Inkbound prototype, directed and reviewed by Marc Watkins. Symbols, map exports and world files are yours to reuse.
